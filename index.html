Detailed information about marine collagen, ingredients, and the science behind it.

Korean Marine Collagen Peptides are short chains of amino acids derived from fish skin or scales. They're rich in Type I & III collagen, which is essential for healthy skin, hair, nails, and joints. They're known for their high bioavailability, making them one of the best collagen supplements. Marine collagen has a smaller peptide size, making it more bioavailable and faster absorbing than bovine collagen, with superior benefits for skin hydration, elasticity and anti-aging.
Collagen peptides improve skin elasticity by stimulating natural collagen production. This helps reduce wrinkles, fine lines, and sagging. These collagen peptides replenish declining collagen levels and enhance moisture retention, leaving skin looking plumper and more elastic. This is vital for aging or dehydrated skin.
Type I forms 90% and Type III forms 10% in this collagen. Since the skin is made up of Type I collagen, it is the major component. Type I is essential for skin, bones, and tendons, while Type III supports skin elasticity and organ structure. Together they provide comprehensive beauty and structural support.
