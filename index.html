Want to Go Deeper into the Science?

Comprehensive educational information regarding collagen types, peptide absorption, and long-term benefits.

Korean Marine Collagen Peptides are short chains of amino acids derived from deep-sea fish skin or scales. They are rich in Type I & III collagen, which is essential for healthy skin, hair, nails, and joints. Known for high bioavailability, marine collagen peptides have a smaller molecular size than bovine collagen, allowing faster absorption and superior skin hydration and anti-aging benefits.
Collagen peptides replenish declining natural collagen levels and enhance moisture retention, leaving skin looking plumper and more elastic. By stimulating fibroblast activity, they support skin structure from within, helping to reduce fine lines, smooth wrinkles, and improve overall skin texture over time.
Type I forms 90% and Type III forms 10% in this collagen. Since human skin is primarily made up of Type I collagen, Type I is the major component in our hydrolyzed marine collagen peptides.
Marine collagen has a smaller molecular peptide size, making it more bioavailable and faster absorbing than bovine collagen, with superior benefits for skin hydration, elasticity and anti-aging. Wellbeing Nutrition's Marine Collagen is sourced from deep-sea fish for maximum purity and potency.
